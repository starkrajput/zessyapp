Structural Load Calculator 2026 — Professional Beam, Column & Slab Analysis

Free professional structural load calculator for civil and structural engineers. Calculate bending moments, shear forces, deflections, and perform comprehensive safety checks for beams, columns, and slabs. Free alternative to SkyCiv ($59/month) and ClearCalcs ($49/month). Features include: beam analysis (simply supported, cantilever, continuous, fixed), column design with Euler buckling calculations, slab design for one-way and two-way systems, safety factor verification, professional PDF reports with custom branding, 8 material options including concrete (M25/M30/M40), steel (Grade 250/415/500), and timber. Complete with bending moment diagrams, shear force calculations, deflection analysis, and structural safety checks. Perfect for preliminary design, client presentations, and educational purposes. Used by structural engineers, civil engineers, and construction professionals worldwide. Export calculations to PDF, HTML, or TXT formats with professional templates.
